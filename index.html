FRIENDS

You buy the friend. The friend gets a wallet and one vote. After that it is not really your money.

Every friend ships with a wallet of its own, and the fees from every trade land in that wallet instead of yours. You hold the picture and the picture holds the balance, which is a distinction people tend to discover late.

Every friend also gets exactly one vote on what happens to the pile. Twelve of them are awake so far and they have been voting all week without pausing once to ask how you feel about it.

HOW IT RUNS

Four steps, and only the first one involves you.

STEP 01

THE FRIEND

You pick a friend and it moves in. It is polite about it, it says thank you, and it never once mentions the wallet it arrives with.

STEP 02

THE WALLET

Fees from every trade drip into that wallet all day long. The balance goes up, the friend gets heavier, and nothing about this reaches your side of the screen.

STEP 03

THE VOTE

Once a week the committee sits down and decides what the pile is for. Holders get one vote each, which is exactly as many as every friend in the room already has.

STEP 04

THE SPLIT

Whatever survives the session gets shared out among everyone who was still holding at the gavel. Whatever does not survive is now a rock.
